## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: default
- Creative direction: high-motion, cursor-driven product reel; every feature performed live on screen
- Interpretation: fast eased camera moves (punch-ins, pull-backs, a page-scroll whip, a circle theme-wipe from the real toggle), all on top of real UI. Clicks, toggles and reveals land on the music's strong beats. Text holds long enough to read. The brand stays editorial: paper, ink, one soft yellow.
- Angle: it looks like a cinema seat picker, but the seats are parking bays. A cursor then drives the whole app: theme flip, 3D, lens X-ray, booking, bay pass, both themes side by side.
- Hook: tight on the hero seat map (rows A/C with "SERVICE ROAD · ALL BUMPERS THIS WAY" as the screen). The cursor clicks a pale seat on the 1.60s strong beat and it pops yellow; the camera rips back to the full hero headline.
- Outro / punchline: `arrivo.` slams in, the dot dropping on the 22.65s strong beat, then "Park like you booked a seat." and "KIET, Ghaziabad · first lot mapped".
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS language
  - Abstract filler visuals
  - Unrelated visual redesign

## Hyperframes Instructions
Built with the Hyperframes domain skills (core, animation, creative, keyframes, cli) as one standalone `index.html` with a single paused GSAP timeline. Camera moves are transforms on page-sized wrappers; the lens magnifier is driven by CSS custom properties so the glass, its X-ray content, the readout and the cursor share one path. `hyperframes check` is the gate before render.
