# Brag Plan: Arrivo

## What is this app?
Arrivo books a parking bay ahead of time the way you'd pick a cinema seat: a live, bay-by-bay plan of the KIET main lot (96 bays, 5 rows) where you tap a bay, see who has it and until when, pick your hours and get a bay pass.

## The angle
"It looks like a seat picker. The seats are parking bays." The video opens on what reads as a cinema seat-selection screen. The service road is labelled where the screen would be: "ALL BUMPERS THIS WAY". Then it pulls back to reveal the real headline, *Park like you booked a seat.*

From there a cursor drives the whole product live: flipping light to dark, tilting the lot into 3D, dragging the magnifier across the plan, booking a bay and getting the pass. Each feature is performed, not listed, and theme flips are the cuts between them.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Tight on the hero seat map (rows A and C, pale free seats, dark parked cars, hatched reserved ones). A cursor glides in and clicks a pale seat on the first strong beat (1.60s), and it pops yellow. The camera then rips back to the full hero: **"Park like you booked a seat."**

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Theme flip:** the cursor hits the nav toggle and a circle of dark mode floods out from it across the hero (strong cue 3.70s).
- **3D:** in dark mode on the live lot, the cursor clicks **3D** and the plan tilts into a model with buildings standing up.
- **The lens:** after a flip back to light, the cursor picks up the magnifier and drags it across the bays. Inside the glass the lot turns to X-ray (yellow free, black booked, hatched reserved) with bay IDs and times. A readout card follows: "C-07 · Free … let go to open C-07". Releasing it opens the bay.
- **Booking:** the camera punches into the panel. The cursor picks **3 h**, types the plate `UP14 AB 1234` key by key, and hits **Hold C-07**. The bay pass flips in (strong cue 17.91s).
- **Every bay, one list:** a split screen sweeps across *All 96 bays*, light on one side and dark on the other.

## Outro / punchline
Everything pulls away and **arrivo.** slams in with its yellow dot on the 22.65s strong cue. Under it: *Park like you booked a seat.* and the mono line *KIET, Ghaziabad · first lot mapped*.

## User flow worth showing
Pick a bay (tap on the map, or sweep the lens and let go) → choose hours and type your plate → "Hold C-07" → bay pass ("Held for you").

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: high-motion, cursor-driven product reel; every feature performed live on screen
- Interpretation: fast camera moves, whips and wipes on the beat, with real UI everywhere and no abstract filler. Text holds long enough to read (the headline holds about 2.4s). The motion is energetic, but the brand stays calm and editorial (paper, ink, one yellow).

## Audio direction
- Role: dense-ish rhythmic layer that rides the edit (high-motion reel)
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11 (114.8 BPM, punchy from the first beat, a short breath around 15-17s that suits the typing beat)
- Music treatment: start at 0, bed around 0.36, fade out over the last ~1.2s under the logo
- Music cue guidance: preset `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`. Strong cue locks: **1.60** (seat click), **3.70** (theme flip), **17.91** (bay pass). Also near strong beats: 6.34 (3D), 12.65 (lens release), 22.65 (logo). Beat grid every ~0.523s for cursor clicks and sequential items.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. Bass makes the soft yellow glow behind the outro wordmark breathe. No visualisers.
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched. Clicks on every cursor click, a switch on theme toggles, soft whooshes/slides on camera whips, single keypresses while typing the plate, a drop when the lens is picked up, one bell for the bay pass and one for the logo.
- Audio-coupled moments: seat click, toggle flips, 3D click, lens pick-up/release, typed plate, Hold button, ticket flip, logo slam.
- Restraint rule: no stacked hits on the same frame and no sound on purely decorative motion. Keep keypresses quiet (≈0.5).

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Seat pick — 0.00–3.18s
Tight on the real hero seat map (light): rows A and C as seats, the service road labelled "ALL BUMPERS THIS WAY" like a cinema screen. The cursor glides in and clicks a pale seat at 1.60s; it pops yellow with a ripple. The camera pulls back hard to the full hero, revealing **arrivo.**, the nav and the headline *Park like you booked a seat.*
Sequential/interaction: yes, a simulated cursor click on a seat.
Audio intent: playful "tick" into the first downbeat.
Audio-coupled idea: mouse click on the seat (beat-locked 1.60), whoosh on the pull-back.
Music: punchy bed from 0.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Lights off — 3.18–5.28s
Full hero stays put (the headline must read: it holds from about 2.9 to 5.28s). The cursor flies to the nav theme switch and clicks at 3.70s. A circle of dark mode expands from the switch across the whole page, giving the same hero in dark.
Sequential/interaction: yes, a toggle click.
Audio intent: a satisfying mechanical switch.
Audio-coupled idea: switch sound beat-locked to 3.70.
Transition mood: dramatic (circle wipe) → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The lot, in 3D — 5.28–8.44s
A whip down from the hero to the live lot (dark), landing on the 5.80 beat: the plan of all 96 bays beside the side panel. The cursor clicks **3D** (≈6.34) and the view swaps to the tilted model with a camera push.
Sequential/interaction: yes, the cursor clicks the 3D segment.
Audio intent: momentum, a whoosh into the lot, then a click.
Audio-coupled idea: slide/whoosh on the whip, click on 3D.
Transition mood: hard → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The lens — 8.44–12.90s
The cursor hits the theme switch (8.44): a circle wipe back to light, on the plan view. The cursor grabs the magnifier (≈8.96, lens lifts) and drags it across the rows. Inside the glass the lot is X-rayed (free = yellow, booked = black, IDs + times). A readout card rides beside it, landing on "C-07 · Free · … · let go to open C-07". Release at 12.65: the bay opens (panel shows C-07, yellow crosshair guides on the plan).
Sequential/interaction: yes, press, drag and release the lens.
Audio intent: tactile pick-up, a soft glide, a decisive drop.
Audio-coupled idea: drop/pick-up sound, a release click near 12.65.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Book it — 12.90–18.44s
The camera punches into the panel. The cursor clicks **3 h** (price updates), clicks the vehicle field and types **UP14 AB 1234** (one keypress per character), then clicks **Hold C-07**. A holding bar fills, and the **bay pass** flips in at 17.91: yellow card, big bay ID, arrive/leave, vehicle and barcode. "Held for you." holds.
Sequential/interaction: yes, a chip click, typed plate and a button press. Typed text gets a keypress per character, about 0.11s apart.
Audio intent: tight, precise, then a reward bell on the pass.
Audio-coupled idea: keypresses, a button click, bell beat-locked to 17.91.
Transition mood: hard → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Every bay, both themes — 18.44–21.59s
*All 96 bays*: the real list with status dots, "booked till", time-left bars and today strips. It pans down while a diagonal divider sweeps across, light on the left and dark on the right, so both themes show at once.
Sequential/interaction: the divider sweep and the list pan.
Audio intent: a sweep that rides the beat.
Audio-coupled idea: a slide sound on the divider sweep.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 7

### Scene 7 — arrivo. — 21.59–24.60s
The UI pulls away to paper. **arrivo.** slams in at 22.65 with the yellow dot dropping into place. Below: *Park like you booked a seat.* and *KIET, Ghaziabad · first lot mapped*. Hold, while the music fades out.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: confident landing.
Audio-coupled idea: bell on the logo (22.65), music fade.
